# Disk pressure
Version: 1

## Signals
No space left on device; disk full; disk_usage_percent

## Diagnostics and uncertainty
Inspect usage and inode counts. A threshold breach does not mean 100% utilization. Deletion and retention changes need operator review.
Correlate event timing and deployment changes. Do not execute remediation automatically.
